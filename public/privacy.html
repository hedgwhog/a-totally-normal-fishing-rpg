Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Who is responsible

This game is run by Lucas Bouwmeester, who is responsible for your data. Hosting country is Sweden. Questions, or want something changed or deleted? Email hedgwhog@gmail.com.

Age (Restrictions)

You must be at least 13 years old to make an account. We ask for your date of birth to check this. The reason behind this is, that you can only play with others (chat, voice, friends, letters, trading and duels), if you are 16 or above. Younger players play alone, and have no way of contacting others in game. If you tried to fill in an account while under 13 years of age, no account is made and nothing is stored about you. (only your connection for one day, so you can't directly try a different date)

What is stored, and why

Your account: your username, display name, date of birth, the date you signed up, which version of the Terms of Service you accepted, when you accepted that verson of the Terms of Service, your titles (and which one you wear), and your password. The password is only stored as a scrambled hash with a unique salt, so nobody can read it, including us. Your logins are also only stored as a hash. We don't ask for your email address.

Your progress: every account has 3 saves. A save has your items, fish, coins, resonance, gear, map, quests, the name of your carrier pigeon, how long you played, which town you're in, and which areas you've been to.

Friends and letters your friend list, and the letters your friends send you (the message, who sent it, from which town, and the gifts that came with it).

Chat: your messages, with your display name, the time and the area. Players in the same area aged 16 and older can read them, except players who blocked you. A filter replaces some bad words with ***.

Blocked players: the players you block are saved in your account until you unblock them. Blocked players are not told.

Voice chat: voice is never recorded or stored. It goes directly between the players in your voice room. A closed room's password is only kept in the server's memory while the room exists. The game doesn't show your IP address, but the players in your room receive it to connect to each other, so someone with technical knowledge can find it. To connect, your browser also contacts a STUN server (Google), which sees your IP address. This only happens when you join a voice room, and the game warns you first. If you send us your own recording of voice chat as proof for a report, we only use it for that report, and delete it when the report is handled.

Reports: when you report a message, a letter or a player, we store who reported it, who was reported, the rule, what you wrote, and the reported message with the chat around it (or the reported letter), so the admins can see what happened. The reported player is not told who reported them.

Moderation: when an admin acts (a warning, timeout, ban, deleting a message or an account), we store what was done, by which admin, to which account, why, and until when. This is shown to the player it is about.

IP address: to slow down bots and password guessing, your IP address is used to count attempts when you log in or sign up. This only happens in the server's memory: it's never saved, and is forgotten within a day. The hosting provider (cPanel) may also keep standard server logs.

Your browser: the game stores your login, your settings (volume, colors, keybinds, animations) and which notices you've already seen in your browser's local storage. This is only needed to make the game work. There are no tracking cookies, no ads and no analytics, so there's no cookie banner.

Why (legal basis)

  • To run the game you signed up for: your account, progress, friends, letters, chat and voice (GDPR article 6(1)(b), contract).
  • To keep the game safe and fair: the age check, the word filter, reports, moderation, the moderation log, and counting log-in and sign-up attempts against bots (GDPR article 6(1)(f), legitimate interest: protecting players, especially young ones).
  • When the law requires it, for example passing information about a serious threat to someone's life or safety to the police (GDPR article 6(1)(c), legal obligation).

Nothing is sold and nothing is used to decide things about you automatically. The word filter only replaces words with ***.

Who else can see it

Other players see your display name, your chat messages (16+), and the letters you send them. Players in your voice room receive your IP address. Your friends see on their start screen, if you are online. The admins of the game can see accounts, chat messages, reports and the moderation log. So they can moderate the game. The hosting provider (cPanel) stores the game's data on its servers. Google's STUN server sees your IP address when you use voice chat. We give information to the police only when there is a serious threat, or when the law requires it.

How long it's kept

  • Chat messages: 18 hours. A reported message and the chat around it are kept with the report (see below).
  • Reports: until an admin has handled them, and then 90 days.
  • The moderation log: 1 year.
  • Counting log-in and sign-up attempts (with your IP address): in memory only, at most 1 day.
  • Logins: expire after 30 days.
  • Letters: until the person who got them throws them away. Every player keeps at most 30, the oldest are deleted first.
  • Your progress in a save: until you reset it (Settings in the game) or delete your account.
  • Your account, date of birth and block list: until you delete your account.

Updates of the game can change or remove things in your saves (see the Terms of Service).

Your rights

You can delete your account yourself (Account button, top left on the menu screen). This deletes your account, saves, friends, letters and chat messages. You can also ask for a copy of your data (you get one file with everything we have about you). You can ask to have it corrected or deleted and object to how it's used. Email hedgwhog@gmail.com. You'll get an answer within 1 month. Not happy with the answer? You can complain to the data protection authority in your country.

If something goes wrong

If there's ever a data breach, we report it to the data protection authority as the law requires, and we tell the players about it.. The game shows an emergency notice to everyone when that's needed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes are announced on the introduction page and on GitHub, and in the game when needed.
