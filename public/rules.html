Rules

Last updated: 3 November 2026

The following rules are for the chat, voice chat, letters, names, trades and duels. They are here, so that everyone can have a good time. (playing with others are for players aged 16 and above.)

  1. Be respectful:Harassment, bullying, insults, threats, and discrimination (that includes racism, sexism, homophobia, and transphobia) will not be tolerated.
  2. Be appropriate:Keep all content appropriate for all audiences. Sexual messages, names, jokes, and explicit material are strictly forbidden.
  3. Respect privacy:Don't share personal information and respect each others privacy. Do not share or ask real names, adresses, phone numbers, social media accounts, and photos.
  4. No Spam and related:Spam, advertising, scams and hyperlinks are strictly forbidden.
  5. Play fair:Cheating, using bots, and exploiting bugs are strictly forbidden. If you discover a bug please report it immediately.
  6. Don't force believes:Do not act badly upon players with different believes. (for example, politics and religion)
  7. Authenticity:Do not impersonate admins, moderators, and other players in general. Usernames and Displaynames must also comply with all community guidelines.
  8. Legal issues:Do not share illegal content or encourage activities that are dangerous or unlawfu.
  9. Age Restrictions:You must be 13 or older to play, and tell the truth about your age. Playing with others (chat, voice, friends, letters, trading and duels), are only for players aged 16 and above.
  10. Language Policy:To help moderation and to keep the community safe, all text communication, voice chat, letters, and usernames must be in English only.

What happens when someone breaks a rule?

Depending on the situation, the admins can:

  • Hand out a warning
  • Delete messages
  • Time out the chat for someone
  • Time out the game for someone
  • Ban an account (for a set period or forever)
  • Delete an account

You are always told the reason, what happened, what rule was broken and until when. Think it's a mistake? Email: hedgwhog@gmail.com and we'll take a look at it again.

There is a filter replaces some obvious bad words with '***', and hides links, email addresses and phone numbers. But it won't be able to catch everything, so the rules always apply.

Voice chat

The rules count in voice chat as well. But voice is never recorded or stored by the game itself, so the admins can't hear what was said afterwards, which makes it hard to moderate. Did someone break the rules in voice, and do you have your own recording of it? Email it to hedgwhog@gmail.com. There is always the option to leave a voice room and block the person in the chat.

Serious threats

When a message suggests that someone's life or safety is in danger (for example a threat of violence, or someone who might hurt themselves), we may contact the police and give them the information they need. For example their messages and the account involved. The law may require us to do this.

Is someone in danger right now? Call your local emergency number first!

Reporting

See something that breaks the rules? Use the Report button next to a message in the chat to report them. You can do the same in the players tab and the same procedure counts for a letter as well. Don't have an account, or a complaint about content that belongs to you, or is it about something else? Email hedgwhog@gmail.com.
