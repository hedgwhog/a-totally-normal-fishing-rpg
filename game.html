Some explantion of how different mechanics work.

                
Fishing:

                
To catch fish, you need to time catching the fish. Depending on what fish, the fish can be faster, smaller and rarer to get.

                
Some fishes can only be catched in a certain habitat, so you need to have the correct fishing rod to be able to catch them. Every habitat has their own lootpool.

                
You can sell your catched fish for coins, by selecting the amount and type with the "+" and "-" keys, and then selling it to the fishmonger.

                
You can fish other items up, but those are quite rare.

                
                
Marketplace:

                
You can upgrade your gear here for coins. It is also the place to sell old gear you have no interest in. You can also buy a different type of fishing rods here. _______ .Keep scrolling. 

                
The following upgrades could also be included in a fishing rod:

                
 - Focus: By focussing, it seems like the fish swim slower.

                
 - Luck: Makes rarer fish magically show up more.


                
Resonance:

                
You can resonance your fishing rod to make it better. You get a random upgrade, and depending on the level of resonance, it can be a lot better.
