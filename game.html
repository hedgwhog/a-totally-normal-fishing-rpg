Fishing:

                
To catch a fish, press SPACE (or click the button) when your line | is on the fish (===). The fish moves as well.

                
Every fish is different: its speed, the size, the length and its value on the market. You need a certain amount of hits in a certain amount of rounds.

                
Some fish can only be caught in a certain habitat, so you need to have the correct fishing rod to be able to catch them. Every habitat has its own lootpool. (NOTE: The habitat automatically changes depending on the fishing rod you currently have equipped)

                
You can sell your fish to The Fishmonger for coins. You can choose the amount you want to sell by using the "+" and "-" icons.


                
Marketplace:

                
Over here you can buy new gear and sell your old gear.


                
Resonance:

                
At the resonance altar you can "resonate" your fishing rod to make it better. The resonance you get are random. A higher rank costs more, but gives a bigger chance on stronger and multiple resonances.

                
Currently available:

                
 - FOCUS: By focusing, your fishing line moves slower, which makes it easier to time.

                
 - LUCK: You're more likely to hook more valuable fish.

                
 - HITBOX: The fish becomes easier to hit. Every level makes its zone 1 bigger.

                
 - REWIND: Rewind after a miss and try again (except the last round!). Every level gives you 1 rewind per fish.

                
A rod with upgrades can't be resonated again. Use DISSONANCE to remove all its upgrades first, if you want to reroll them.
