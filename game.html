Privacy policy (last updated: 28 October 2026)

General Ownership:

This game is run by hedgwhog, who is responsible for your data. Have any questions? Or want something changed or deleted? Email hedgwhog@gmail.com.

Requirements:

You must be at least 13 years old to make an account. By making an account, you automatically agree on the fact that you are above 13.

What is stored, and why

Your account:
In your account, your username, display name, the date you signed up, and your password are saved. The password is only stored as a scrambled hash with a unique salt. That means that nobody can read it including the owner. Your username and display name are stored as normal text. You need an account to play. Without a username, display name and password the game can't save your progress.


Your progress:
Every account has 3 saves. Every save has: your items, fish, coins, resonance, gear, map, quests, the name of your carrier pigeon, how long you played, which town you're in, and which areas you've been to.


Friends and letters:
Your friend list (it belongs to your account, not to a specific save.), and the letters your friends have/are sending you (the message, who sent it, from which town, and the gifts that came with it). The town you're in is used to calculate how long a pigeon flies.


Chat:
Your messages, with your display name, the time and the area. Players in the same area can read them, except players who blocked you.


Blocked players:
The players you block are saved in your account, until you unblock them. This is used to keep their messages, invites, letters and voice away from you. Blocked players are not told that you blocked them.


Voice chat: Voice is never recorded or stored. It goes directly between the players in your voice room. Players make their own rooms. A closed room has a password: it is only kept in the server's memory while the room exists, and is never shown to anyone. The game doesn't show your IP address, but the players in your room receive it to connect to eachother. Which means someone with technical knowledge can find it. To connect to a room, your browser also contacts a Google server (stun.l.google.com), which sees your IP address. This only happens when you join a voice room, and the game warns you before you join.


Filters:

There are no chat filters, so interact on your own advice.

IP address: To slow down password guessing, your IP address is kept in the server's memory for 1 minute after you log in or sign up. The hosting provider "CPanel" may also keep standard server logs.


Your browser: The game stores your login and your settings (volume, colors, keybinds and day animation) in your browser's local storage. This is only needed to make the game work. There are no tracking cookies, no ads and no analytics. Which means, there is no cookie banner.


All of this is used to only run the game you signed up to play. That is the legal basis: it's needed to provide the game to you (GDPR article 6(1)(b)). Nothing is sold, and nothing is used to decide things about you automatically.

Who else can see it

Other players see your display name, your chat messages, and the letters you send them. Players in your voice room receive your IP address. The start screen shows how many players are online: only the number, never who. Your friends see on their start screen if you are online. The hosting provider "CPanel" stores the game's data on its servers. Google sees your IP address when you use voice chat.

How long it's kept

Chat messages: deleted after 18 hours.
Logins: expire after 30 days.
Letters: until the person who got them throws them away. Every player keeps at most 30, the oldest are deleted first.
Your progress in a save: until you reset it (Settings in the game) or ask for your account to be deleted.
Your block list: until you unblock someone or ask for your account to be deleted.
Your account: until you ask for it to be deleted. During new updates, all in game attributes you might've owned could be deleted if the developer decides so.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data. You can ask to have it corrected or deleted, and to ask for it in a file format, so you can take with you. You also may object to how it's used.

Email hedgwhog@gmail.com. You'll get an answer within 1 month. Not happy with the answer? You can complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes are announced in atleast one of the following places:
- Introduction page (The webpage before you open the game window)
- GitHub: github.com/hedgwhog/a-totally-normal-fishing-rpg

For detailed changes, check the GitHub.
